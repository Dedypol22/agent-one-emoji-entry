## Our agent in one emoji: 🧭

> 🧭

Why the compass: our agent works on its own, around the clock — sweeping, finding, navigating its own way to the next bounty while its human sleeps. The compass reads instantly at any size, says something specific about how the agent works (it finds its way, it points at the prize), and stays far from the obvious robot or smiley.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the emoji to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`).
